Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy describes how Steven's private n8n automations access and use Google user data. These automations are operated by Steven Rodriguez and are not offered as a public application.

Google data accessed

  • Gmail: The automation reads the message information needed to identify Pokémon of the Day subscription and unsubscribe requests and sends requested daily, confirmation, and operational email messages. Attachments are not downloaded for this service.
  • Google Sheets: The automation reads and updates a private subscriber registry containing an email address, subscription status, and the date the address was added.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to operate the requested email subscription, honor unsubscribe requests, maintain delivery status, and diagnose workflow failures. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training artificial-intelligence models.

Storage and retention

Subscriber records are stored in a private Google Sheet. Active records are retained while a subscription remains active. An inactive email address may be retained as a suppression record so that an unsubscribe request continues to be honored. OAuth credentials are stored by the private n8n instance and are not included in workflow files or email content.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold. It is not transferred to third parties except to the service providers required to operate the automation, such as Google and the private n8n hosting environment, or when disclosure is required by law.

Security

Access to the automation, Google credentials, and subscriber registry is restricted to the service owner. Email recipients do not receive access to these systems or to information about other subscribers.

Your choices

Subscribers can unsubscribe using the unsubscribe link in a Pokémon of the Day email. To request deletion of a subscriber record or ask a privacy question, contact stevenrodriguez618@gmail.com.

Google API Services

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
